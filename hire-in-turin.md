# Hire in Turin, Italy

> Hiring in Turin for Nordic companies: automotive and aerospace engineering, universities, local income tax surcharges and practical notes.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Hiring by city</span> <h1 id="hire-in-turin">Hire in Turin</h1> <p class="lede">Turin built the Italian car industry and still designs and tests much of it. For Nordic vehicle, truck and component makers, it is the obvious place to find engineers who already know Italian suppliers, at lower cost than Milan.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="sectors">Sectors</h3><p>Automotive and its supply chain, aerospace and space systems, industrial automation, ICT and software, food and beverage brands, banking. Electrification and software-defined vehicles are where Nordic and Piedmontese engineering overlap most.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="where-people-study">Where people study</h3><p>Politecnico di Torino for mechanical, aerospace, electronic and computer engineering; the Università di Torino for sciences, economics and law. Many engineers have years inside large industrial groups.</p></div> </section> <section> <h2 id="local-income-tax-in-turin">Local income tax in Turin</h2> <p class="prose">On top of national IRPEF, employees pay a city and a regional surcharge, both withheld through payroll. A senior engineer in Turin pays more city surcharge than one in Milan or Bologna at the same gross. Cheaper housing usually offsets it.</p>  </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="practical-notes">Practical notes</h2> <ul>  <li><b>Commuting.</b> One metro line, trams, buses and a suburban rail link. Flat and compact, with many cyclists, which Nordic managers tend to like.</li> <li><b>Travel.</b> High-speed trains reach Milan in about an hour. For Nordic flights, connect through a hub or Milan Malpensa.</li>  <li><b>Time zone.</b> Italy runs on the same clock as Stockholm, Oslo and Copenhagen, and one hour behind Helsinki.
