# Hire in Naples, Italy

> Hiring in Naples for Nordic companies: aerospace, shipping, developer talent, universities, local income tax and practical notes.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Hiring by city</span> <h1 id="hire-in-naples">Hire in Naples</h1> <p class="lede">Naples has a large graduate pool, lower living costs than the north and a port that matters to Nordic shipping and logistics companies. It works well for engineering, support and developer teams that do not need to sit next to clients.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="sectors">Sectors</h3><p>Aerospace and aircraft components, shipping and port logistics, food production, pharma, tourism, and software teams around the Apple Developer Academy and the San Giovanni a Teduccio campus.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="where-people-study">Where people study</h3><p>Federico II, one of the oldest public universities in the world, for engineering, computer science and medicine; Parthenope for economics and maritime studies; L'Orientale for languages. Many graduates have moved north and would return for the right job.</p></div> </section> <section> <h2 id="local-income-tax-in-naples">Local income tax in Naples</h2> <p class="prose">On top of national IRPEF, employees pay a city and a regional surcharge, both withheld through payroll. At equal gross, take-home pay is lower than in Milan or Bologna. Housing costs reverse much of that gap.</p>  </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="practical-notes">Practical notes</h2> <ul>  <li><b>Commuting.</b> Metro lines, funiculars and the Circumvesuviana railway.
