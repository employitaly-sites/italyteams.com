# Hire in Bologna, Italy

> Hiring in Bologna for Nordic companies: packaging machinery, motor brands, data centres, universities, local income tax and practical notes.

Nordic packaging, food-processing and automation companies buy from, compete with and sell to the firms around it, which makes it a natural place for a field or applications engineer.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="sectors">Sectors</h3><p>Packaging and automatic machinery (the "Packaging Valley"), motorcycles and sports cars, mechatronics, food processing, logistics, and high-performance computing around the Tecnopolo and CINECA.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="where-people-study">Where people study</h3><p>The Università di Bologna, one of the largest in Italy, for engineering, computer science, economics and agriculture; nearby universities in Modena, Reggio Emilia, Ferrara and Parma add mechanical and automotive engineers.</p></div> </section> <section> <h2 id="local-income-tax-in-bologna">Local income tax in Bologna</h2> <p class="prose">On top of national IRPEF, employees pay a city and a regional surcharge, both withheld through payroll.
