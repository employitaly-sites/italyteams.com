# Hire Software Engineers in Italy

> Hiring developers in Italy for Nordic tech companies: CCNL, roles, where engineers live, remote work in the same time zone, IP and cost.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Hiring by sector</span> <h1 id="software-engineers-in-italy">Software engineers in Italy</h1> <p class="lede">For a Stockholm, Oslo or Copenhagen scale-up, Italy is a near-shore market in the same time zone. Engineers are well trained, many already work remotely for foreign companies, and salaries are lower than at home. Most hires will be fully remote, which shapes the contract.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="usual-ccnl">Usual CCNL</h3><p>Commerce and services (Terziario) or Metalworking (Metalmeccanico) for tech companies, depending on your activity and the role.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="roles">Roles</h3><p>Backend, frontend and mobile developers, DevOps and SRE, data and ML engineers, QA, engineering managers.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="where">Where</h3><p>Milan, Turin, Rome, Bologna, Pisa and Florence, Naples. Many seniors have left the big cities and work remotely from smaller towns.</p></div> </section>  <section class="prose"> <h2 id="points-to-settle">Points to settle</h2> <ul> <li><b>Remote work is a written agreement.</b> Italian "lavoro agile" needs one, with rules on equipment and the right to disconnect. We add it to the contract.</li> <li><b>Equipment.</b> Ship a laptop or let us reimburse one. Documented either way, so it is not taxed as salary.</li> <li><b>IP.</b> Code written under employment belongs to the employer. Our explicit IP and confidentiality clause assigns it to your company.</li>  <li><b>On-call and overtime.</b> On-call is paid under the CCNL. For senior engineers, a "quadro" contract with flat-rate extra hours is an option.</li> <li><b>Flat hierarchy, written decisions.</b> Italian engineers adapt quickly to Nordic consensus culture, but expect decisions and priorities in writing, not only agreed in a meeting.</li> </ul> </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="cost-example">Cost example</h2>  <div class="cta"><a class="btn" href="calculator.html">Open the calculator</a><a class="btn ghost" href="contact.html">Hire an engineer</a></div> </section>
