# Hire in Milan, Italy

> Hiring in Milan for Nordic companies: sectors, universities, local income tax surcharges, take-home pay and practical notes.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Hiring by city</span> <h1 id="hire-in-milan">Hire in Milan</h1> <p class="lede">Milan is where Italian buyers, design studios and head offices sit. For a Nordic company it is usually the first Italian seat: one sales lead, one designer, one engineer. The market is quick, and good candidates have several options.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="sectors">Sectors</h3><p>Fashion, furniture and industrial design, banking and asset management, consulting, media, pharma and the Italian head offices of most multinationals. Fintech and SaaS startups cluster around the city, and many Nordic consumer and design brands run their Italian sales from here.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="where-people-study">Where people study</h3><p>Politecnico di Milano for engineering and design, Bocconi for economics and management, the Università Statale for law, sciences and medicine, Bicocca for sciences and statistics.</p></div> </section> <section> <h2 id="local-income-tax-in-milan">Local income tax in Milan</h2> <p class="prose">On top of national IRPEF, employees pay a city and a regional surcharge, both withheld through payroll. Lombardy also starts with a low regional rate. At equal gross pay, a Milan employee normally keeps slightly more than one in Rome or Naples. Rent eats part of that advantage.</p>  </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="practical-notes">Practical notes</h2> <ul>  <li><b>Commuting.</b> Five metro lines, suburban rail and trams.
