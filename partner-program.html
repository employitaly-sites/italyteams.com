Partner programme

Nordic advisors, Italian teams

Auditors and accountants, law firms, HR consultants, trade councils, chambers of commerce and resellers in Sweden, Norway, Denmark and Finland: when a client wants engineers or sales staff in Italy, ItalyTeams employs them as Employer of Record (EOR) and you remain the adviser they trust at home.

Why Nordic companies skip the subsidiary

A Nordic scale-up hiring three people in Turin does not need an Italian board, a local auditor and a year of filings. With ItalyTeams, the people sign an Italian employment contract, are paid in euro on time and are covered by the right collective agreement. The client keeps managing them in its own way. €250 per employee per month, with no extras.

What you get as a partner

Auditors, lawyers and HR consultants

  • Fast access for Nordic professionals: one contact person who knows your cases.
  • A referral fee per client, or a discount for the client: you decide.
  • Workshops for your team on how Italian employment differs from Nordic practice.
  • A calculator with your branding to compare Italian and Nordic employer cost.
  • When the client opens an Italian company, you or your network handle it, and we charge no exit penalty.
  • Short updates on Italian contracts, contributions, visas and deadlines, ready to share.

Trade councils and chambers

  • Discounts of up to 20% for your member companies, linked to volume.
  • Support in the language your members prefer, with live translation.
  • Joint guides and webinars, talks at your Italy events and a partner page with a link to you.
  • Quicker onboarding and visa handling for your companies.
  • Introductions to Italian regions, hiring incentives, recruiters and relocation help.

Resellers in the Nordics

  • White-label or co-branded resale, payslips carrying your logo, on a dedicated reseller rate.
  • A partner manager who replies within one working day and quotes within 24 hours.
  • A partner area for protected leads, client status, monthly reports and material.

Questions Nordic partners ask

Do I stay the client's main adviser?

Yes. We look after employment in Italy only. Accounting, tax and legal work remain yours, and we redirect those questions to you.

Could you end up competing with us?

No. We give no tax or legal advice in the Nordics or in Italy.

How do you protect confidentiality?

A confidentiality clause in the agreement, GDPR-compliant processing and data stored in the EU.

What languages do you use?

English with your clients, Italian and English with employees, and live translation if a client prefers Swedish, Norwegian, Danish or Finnish.

How soon can a client hire?

EU and EEA citizens can usually start within days of the offer. Other nationalities follow the permit timeline we set out in the quote.

When should a client set up in Italy?

Once the Italian team is large or it needs to sell and invoice locally. We then move employees across, and the set-up is your mandate.
