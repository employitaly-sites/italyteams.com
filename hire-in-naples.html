Hiring by city

Hire in Naples

Naples has a large graduate pool, lower living costs than the north and a port that matters to Nordic shipping and logistics companies. It works well for engineering, support and developer teams that do not need to sit next to clients.

Sectors

Aerospace and aircraft components, shipping and port logistics, food production, pharma, tourism, and software teams around the Apple Developer Academy and the San Giovanni a Teduccio campus.

Where people study

Federico II, one of the oldest public universities in the world, for engineering, computer science and medicine; Parthenope for economics and maritime studies; L'Orientale for languages. Many graduates have moved north and would return for the right job.

Local income tax in Naples

On top of national IRPEF, employees pay a city and a regional surcharge, both withheld through payroll. It changes the employee's net pay, not your cost.

Effect on net pay

Naples has the highest flat city rate of the five and a low exemption, and Campania starts high on the regional side too. At equal gross, take-home pay is lower than in Milan or Bologna. Housing costs reverse much of that gap.

Practical notes

  • Commuting. Metro lines, funiculars and the Circumvesuviana railway. Traffic is heavy: put the office near a station.
  • Time zone. Italy runs on the same clock as Stockholm, Oslo and Copenhagen, and one hour behind Helsinki. Your stand-up, your lunch and your Friday afternoon line up without effort; only Reykjavik is off, by one hour in winter and two in summer.
