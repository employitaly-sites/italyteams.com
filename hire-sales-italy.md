# Hire Sales Staff in Italy

> A first seller in Italy for a Nordic company: CCNL, commission plans, company cars, regions, and cost.


<section> <span class="eyebrow">Hiring by sector</span> <h1 id="your-first-seller-in-italy">Your first seller in Italy</h1> <p class="lede">Italian buyers want to meet someone local who speaks their language and knows their industry. For many Nordic companies the first Italian hire is therefore a seller. What needs care: the commission plan, the car and the travel.</p> </section> <section class="grid"> <div class="panel"><h3 id="usual-ccnl">Usual CCNL</h3><p>Commerce and services (Terziario). Senior sales leaders can be hired as quadro or under the executives' agreement.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="roles">Roles</h3><p>Account executives, key account managers, business development, country manager, presales and solution engineers, customer success.</p></div> <div class="panel"><h3 id="where">Where</h3><p>Milan for most B2B buyers and head offices. Rome for public sector, energy and telecoms. Bologna, Turin and the Veneto for industrial customers.</p></div> </section>  <section class="prose"> <h2 id="points-to-settle">Points to settle</h2> <ul> <li><b>Commission is salary.</b> It carries contributions and counts for the TFR. Budget it that way.</li> <li><b>A written plan.</b> Targets, payout dates and what happens on exit, all in writing. Vague plans end in disputes.</li> <li><b>Company car.</b> Normal for field sales. Private use is taxed as a benefit at a flat share, often cheaper than a raise. We handle leasing, fuel cards and tolls.</li> <li><b>Travel.</b> Daily allowances and reimbursed expenses have tax-free limits; we process them in the payslip.</li>  <li><b>Employee, not agent.</b> Italy also has commercial agents with their own contract type. If the person sells only for you and under your direction, employment is the safer set-up.</li> <li><b>Signing contracts.</b> A seller who regularly concludes contracts in your name can create a taxable presence for your company in Italy. Agree with us how authority to sign is handled.</li> </ul> </section> <section class="prose"> <h2 id="cost-example">Cost example</h2>  <div class="cta"><a class="btn" href="calculator.html">Open the calculator</a><a class="btn ghost" href="contact.html">Hire your first seller</a></div> </section>
